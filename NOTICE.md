# Notice — On the Licence

This repository carries no licence in the ordinary sense. It carries a public-domain
dedication: CC0-1.0, no rights reserved.

Earlier releases (v0.1.0, v0.2.0) used an asymmetric arrangement — AGPL-3.0 for code,
CC-BY-4.0 for methodology and schemas. That arrangement was structurally sound inside the
framework it operated in. Copyleft is a precise instrument: it uses copyright, the legal
mechanism developed to enforce enclosure, to enforce the opposite. The four freedoms
propagate downstream because the licence compels them to.

But copyleft is enforced through copyright, copyright is recognised through a legal regime,
and that regime is the institution whose enclosure the position was refusing in the first
place. The architecture of refusal operates by turning the institution's own instrument
against the institution's intended use of it — which works exactly as long as the institution
keeps recognising the instrument, and exactly as long as the holder is willing to invoke it.

Harmonia is not willing to invoke it. That is stated publicly and without qualification at
<https://harmonism.io/transmission>: *Harmonia will not pursue remedy through courts whose
authority the doctrine itself diagnoses as severed from Logos.* A copyleft licence whose
holder has publicly renounced enforcing it is a threat already disarmed. Keeping it would be
theatre.

So the licence goes. What replaces it is not weaker protection but differently grounded
protection.

**The depth of the work.** A fork can copy the schemas. It cannot copy the years of
articulation that produced them, and it diverges the moment it has to extend them.

**The conformance suite.** SDIP conformance is a property an implementation demonstrates,
not a permission a licence grants. A closed harness is non-conforming by definition. No
lawsuit is required to say so.

**The canonical surfaces.** harmonism.io and the named handles are the provenance. When
cryptographic signature on Arweave or comparable substrate lands, the signature is the
provenance.

The cost is named honestly. CC0 permits what AGPL forbade: a company may take this work,
close its copy, and serve it. That is accepted. It is the same trade the corpus already
accepts, on the same ground — a counterfeit exposes itself by its inability to produce what
the real transmission produces.

One caveat of legal fact rather than posture: CC0 waives copyright and related rights; it
does not grant patent rights. Harmonia holds no patents over this work and seeks none.

Two things in Harmonia's operational systems are not commons and never will be: credentials,
and practitioner data — conversation history, Harmonic Profiles, per-practitioner memory.
That is not enclosure. It is the same sovereignty doctrine at the privacy register. That
material belongs to the practitioner, not to Harmonia and not to the public.

— Harmonia
